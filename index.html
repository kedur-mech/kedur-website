Global sourcing

Parts and machinery from around the world

Can't find it in the UK? We source parts, spares and complete machinery directly from manufacturers and specialist distributors across Europe, Asia and beyond — and we also export from the UK to customers overseas.

  • Genuine OEM and equivalent parts, with certification from the manufacturer on request
  • Obsolete and hard-to-find spares tracked down
  • Import, export, shipping and documentation handled for you
  • Ex-works or delivered pricing, quoted clearly up front

Made to order

Specialist parts manufactured on demand

When a part simply isn't available, we have it made. Working from your drawings, specifications or a worn sample, our manufacturing partners produce one-off replacements and small batches.

  • Machined, fabricated and moulded components
  • Reverse engineering from samples
  • Materials and tolerances to your specification
  • One-offs, prototypes and repeat batches
